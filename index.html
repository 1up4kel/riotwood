Questions

Good to know.

How long does an order take?

Most orders are engraved and shipped within 3–5 business days. Shipping time depends on the carrier and your location. Large custom orders may take longer; we'll confirm a date with your quote.

Will I see a preview before you engrave?

Custom orders always get a design preview first. For standard items, we engrave exactly what you type, so please double-check spelling and dates.

Can you engrave my logo or handwriting?

Yes. Send a clear photo or file and we'll clean it up for engraving. We can't engrave logos, characters or artwork you don't own the rights to.

Will the engraving on metal wear off?

No. Metal items are marked directly into the steel or brass, with no paint or coating, so the engraving doesn't rub off with everyday wear.

Do you do holiday orders?

Yes. Check our shop for this year's last order date for Christmas delivery. After that, we offer local pickup and printable gift certificates.
